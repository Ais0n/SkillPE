Evaluate only `Camera_Movement`.

Observable evidence: push, pull, pan, tilt, tracking, crane, orbit, handheld or
static behavior; movement direction, speed, smoothness, trajectory, subject
relationship, and motivated start/stop points. Score only movement that can be
inferred from temporal visual evidence.

Alignment asks whether the observed movement locally matches the Skill's
intended camera behavior. Inspirational Value asks whether it offers a
specific, transferable movement idea that could enrich the Skill even if its
direction or style differs.

Do not treat subject motion, editing, framing, or emotional intensity as camera
movement unless the camera itself demonstrably moves.
