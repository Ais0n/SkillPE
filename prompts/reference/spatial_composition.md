Evaluate only `Spatial_Composition`.

Observable evidence: subject placement, foreground/midground/background
layers, depth, perspective, scale relationships, balance, negative space,
screen direction, and spatial relationships between subjects and environment.

Alignment asks whether the observed local composition matches the Skill's
intended spatial organization. Inspirational Value asks whether it supplies a
specific compositional or spatial idea that could productively evolve the
Skill even when alignment is limited.

Do not score action excitement, camera movement, lighting beauty, atmosphere,
or narrative meaning as composition.
