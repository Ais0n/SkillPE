Evaluate only `Shot_Logic`.

Observable evidence: shot ordering, transitions, continuity, escalation,
reaction/action linkage, temporal rhythm, and how one shot motivates the next.
A single uninterrupted shot can still provide evidence through internal
staging and temporal progression.

Alignment asks whether the observed local shot structure or progression
matches the Skill's shot logic. Inspirational Value asks whether the observed
organization offers a useful alternative sequencing, transition, or rhythmic
idea for evolving the Skill without replacing its core identity.

Do not score camera movement quality, composition, lighting, atmosphere, or
sound design except where they directly establish shot-to-shot or temporal
logic.
