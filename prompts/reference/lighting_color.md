Evaluate only `Lighting_Color`.

Observable evidence: light direction and quality, contrast, exposure,
practicals, silhouettes, highlights and shadows, palette, saturation, color
temperature, and motivated color changes.

Alignment asks whether the observed lighting and color locally match the
Skill's intended treatment. Inspirational Value asks whether the treatment
offers a distinctive and transferable lighting or palette idea for evolving
the Skill even when it differs from the original treatment.

Do not use subject matter, shot logic, camera movement, generic image quality,
or inferred mood as a substitute for concrete lighting/color evidence.
